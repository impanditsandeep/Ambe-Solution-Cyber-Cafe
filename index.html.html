Exam Upload Guidelines & Specifications

Never get your application form rejected. Here are the exact file parameters required by BPSC, SSC & Railway.

Passport Photograph

White or Light background mandatory. Clear ears & face.

File Format: JPG / JPEG
File Size: 20 KB – 50 KB
Dimensions: 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm
Pixel Size: ~200 × 230 Pixels
We resize & edit instantly at Ambe Cafe!

Signature (Sign)

Dark Black or Blue ink pen on plain white paper.

File Format: JPG / JPEG
File Size: 10 KB – 20 KB
Dimensions: 3.5 cm × 1.5 cm
Pixel Size: ~140 × 60 Pixels
High-contrast B/W scanning available!

Certificates & Marksheets

10th, 12th, Graduation, Caste, Niwas or EWS.

File Format: PDF / JPEG
File Size: 100 KB – 300 KB
Resolution: 150 to 200 DPI
Quality: Unblurred Text
Multi-page PDF merger and reducer ready!
